How it works

From scan to paid, in six steps.

Every step is on the guest's own phone, and every step lands on the right staff screen.

01 · SCAN

Each table has its own QR

Guests point their camera at the table card and your menu opens — already knowing which table they're at.

  • Print every table's QR card from the app
  • Replace a code in one tap if a photo of it gets shared
  • Pause QR ordering any time with one switch
02 · MENU

Every dish, the way you'd describe it

Big photos, veg / non-veg marks, spice level, allergens, sizes and extras. Search and a Veg-only filter for the table that asks.

  • Half / Full and Glass / Jug sizes, each with its own price
  • Extras that add to the price — guests can't change what they pay
  • Mark a dish sold out and it greys out instantly
03 · LIVE STATUS

Guests watch their order cook

Received → Cooking → Ready → Served, updating by itself. While they wait, your guest Wi-Fi is one tap away.

  • Phone buzzes when the food is ready
  • Order more any time — it all goes on one bill
  • No “where's my food?” waves across the room
04 · CALL

One tap to call a waiter

Waiter, water, bill or help. The table lights up red on the floor map, and the guest sees “On the way” the moment someone taps On it.

  • Sound alert on every staff phone
  • Who's handling it, and for how long
  • Calls close by themselves when the bill is paid
05 · PAY

Split, tip and pay by UPI

Guests split equally, pick a tip and choose UPI, cash or card. Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm opens with each person's share filled in.

  • Money goes straight to your own UPI ID — no gateway fees
  • GST and service charge worked out for you
  • The waiter confirms with one tap and the table is free
06 · REVIEW

Happy guests go to Google. Unhappy ones talk to you.

After paying, guests rate the visit. Four and five stars are invited to post on Google; anything lower comes to you privately, so you can fix it.

  • Every comment shows on your dashboard
  • Ratings sit on each bill, by table and time

FAQ

Questions owners ask

Do guests need to download an app?

No. The menu opens in the phone's browser straight from the QR code — any Android phone or iPhone.

Do you take a cut of UPI payments?

No. The UPI apps pay straight into your own UPI ID, so there are no gateway fees. The waiter confirms payment with one tap.

What if a guest would rather order from a waiter?

Waiters can take the order on their phone from the same menu. It goes to the same kitchen screen and the same bill.

Can someone order from home using a photo of the QR?

Each table's code can be replaced in one tap, and the old one stops working at once. You can also pause QR ordering for the whole restaurant.

What do I need to start?

A phone or tablet for the kitchen, phones for your waiters, and the QR cards printed from the app. Add your menu and you're ready.

Is guest data safe?

We don't collect any. Orders are linked to a table, not a person. Your staff sign in with their own logins, and each one only sees what their job needs.
